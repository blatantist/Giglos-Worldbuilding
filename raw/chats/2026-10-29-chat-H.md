Here's the Q1 paper, Ryan, Mateas and Wardrip-Fruin, "A Lightweight Videogame Dialogue Manager" (DiGRA/FDG 2016):

- [Abstract page](https://dl.digra.org/index.php/dl/article/view/798)
- [PDF](https://dl.digra.org/index.php/dl/article/download/798/798)
