The future is a narrow, ambiguous slice, so make it an intention/expectation mood ("growing toward"). It's optional and only commits you to a direction, never a certainty.

**Fun rule 3: negation as deflation**
Since negative is described relative to positive, there's no separate negation machinery. A deflation derivation applied to any word gives its absence or decay. One mechanism covers "not," "stagnant," and "sad."
