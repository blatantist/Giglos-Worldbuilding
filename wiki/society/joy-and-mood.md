## Overview

Giglian ethics [centers on "joy"](#^tenet), a healthy outward-growing baseline rather than pleasure. A healthy Giglian [holds a slightly positive baseline as "holding pressure"](#^holding); depression is being [unable to hold pressure, "leaky"](#^leaky). [Speech is controlled giggling](#^speech), and [no negative-valence giggle exists](#^no-neg).

Chat L made pressure more literal. [Speech pressure and body pressure are related, because valence is also physical vigor](#^vigor), and [pressure circulating the air is the Joy mechanism](../biology/organs.md#^joy-circulation), which replaces the older pressure-as-metaphor line (T9, resolved by Zach). [A leak is the process of becoming depressed](#^leak-process), and strained speech comes from being deflated. [Lack of joy is literally having no future](../language/conlang.md#^no-future) in the grammar.

## Canon

- Giglian ethics centers on a tenet translated as "joy", a healthy outward-growing baseline rather than pleasure, and their language has no independent concept of negative emotion. [Z] (D) ^tenet

### Joy and valence

- "Joy" is not necessarily pleasure; it is closer to a healthy baseline where the individual or collective (depending on social hierarchy) can succeed. [Z] (D)
- The word for joy is etymologically muddled like English "happy": pleasure, increased power, needs and wants being met are mixed together, and people argue over what it really means. [Z] (D)
- No real concept of negative emotion. Negatives are described relative to the positive. [Z] (D)
- Metaphor axis is out and in: positive is outward growth, negative is the outcome of no growth (decay, inward). [Z] (D)
- Sadness, misery and depression feel like sinking into yourself, which literally inhibits attention-driven growth. Deep depression means dissolving back into a blob of more neutral cells from lack of effort. Hence joy is physical excellence. [Z] (D)

### Holding pressure

- A healthy Giglian's default is a steady, slightly positive baseline held as "holding pressure". Going below it is not normal and not believed to be intentional; it is an affliction of stagnation, a loss of holding pressure leading to collapse. [Z] (D) ^holding
- Some people manage or afford a better baseline through joyous efforts. [Z] (D)
- Depression is conceptualized as being unable to hold pressure, sometimes described as "leaky". [Z] (D) ^leaky

### Pressure as physiology (L)

- Speech/"breath" pressure can be related to body pressure, because valence is also physical vigor. [Z] (L) ^vigor
  - Breathing and joy: [organs ^joy-circulation](../biology/organs.md#^joy-circulation); faking joy by holding your breath: [organs ^feign-joy](../biology/organs.md#^feign-joy).
- Within the current idea, "leaky" doesn't quite make sense, since an uncontrolled burst is a leak; leaking would be the process of becoming depressed, and strained speech would be strained because they are deflated, low pressure. Zach is not sure how literal that is, but it is at least how it is conceptualized. [Z] (L) ^leak-process
  - Refines [^leaky](#^leaky) (D). Zach, 2026-11-03 ingest review: leaky means someone falling into depression. Not a tension.
  - Bodily deflation as shrinking from atrophy, separate from exhaling: see [conlang ^deflate-vs-exhale](../language/conlang.md#^deflate-vs-exhale).

### Giggling and speech

- Giggling is a general social signal of joyous experience, not human laughter's trust and bonding role. Trust is explicit through shared memory and experience, and hierarchy is stronger because you can know what someone would do. [Z] (D)
- Speech is a controlled extension of giggling; good speech is thought of as controlled laughter. [Z] (D) ^speech
- There is no negative-valence giggle, so Giglians struggle to conceptualize or speak about negative valence. [Z] (D) ^no-neg
- "Giggle" is only the English label for the vocalization type; see [conlang](../language/conlang.md). [Z] (F)

- A tonic, constant low giggle signal for baseline. [C] (D)
- Parallel to computational psychiatry: holding pressure as precision of priors. [C] (D)

### From chat L (Claude)

- The future mood lines up with predictive-processing accounts of depression: the future model flattens, so nothing is expected to grow. [C] (L)
- Pressure as one scalar for shape, growth, giggle threshold and speech clarity. [C] (L)
- Speech as a health readout: exhaustion, injury, hunger and depression all drain the same variable and sound alike, so a worker after a double shift sounds a bit like a depressed person. [C] (L)
- Outskirts speakers, kept hungry on purpose, sound strained; the center hears that as low joy, a personal failing by its own doctrine, giving propaganda an "evidence" channel. [C] (L)
- Splitting relaxes pressure deliberately, so pre-split speech might go soft and slow; elites doing clean splits keep unusually steady articulation. [C] (L)
- Faking a healthy giggle source is a trained elite skill, and listeners learn to hear the seams; strain works as a lie detector. [C] (L)
