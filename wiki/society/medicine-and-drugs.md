## Overview

Giglian medicine is [mostly topical herbalism, constrained by one rule](#^rule): anything that dulls sensation also dulls the attention needed to heal, so [there is no anesthesia](#^no-anesthesia). The [main medicine is an opioid analog](#^opioid) that [hooks blue-collar workers](#^addiction).

## Canon

- Giglian medicine is mostly topical herbalism, constrained by one rule: anything that dulls sensation also dulls the attention the patient needs to heal. [Z] (C) ^rule

### Tools and early technology

- Giglian tool use diverges from human: besides early-tool analogs, they make clothing, ointments and similar to survive other climates. [Z] (C)

### Healing practice

- Lots of herbal medicine around skin issues. [Z] (C)
- Scar removal: cut out small shallow wedges, hold them closed, repeat until all scar tissue is gone. Cuts stay shallow so an ordinary patient can will the wound closed without being distracted. [Z] (C)
- Passive healing exists, attending to the wound speeds it. [Z] (C)
- No anesthesia, not even local, because it dulls the ability to attend to growth. General pain-numbing salves still exist, weak or local enough not to inhibit movement. [Z] (C) ^no-anesthesia

### The main medicine (opioid analog)

- Numbs pain without much affecting attention or sensation, like a drug where you can technically still ambulate but shouldn't drive. [Z] (C) ^opioid
- Blue-collar workers get addicted after overexertion injuries (like hurting your back lifting on the job). Zach called this "beautiful". [Z] (C) ^addiction
- Leaders know its effects, tobacco-industry style, with plausible deniability (see [government](government-and-founder.md)). [Z] (C)

## Unconfirmed

- Moisture tech first (hydrogel wraps, damp fungal mats). [C] (C)
- Ointments as the key invention (sealants, hygroscopic coatings, antifungals). [C] (C)
- Braces and scaffolds to hold a shape without paying attention for it. [C] (C)
- Fungal farming as the founding technology (fungal leather, chitin, glues). [C] (C)
- Ointment supply as a state control lever over who can leave the humid core. [C] (C)
- Ailments: dry rot from desiccation, spore infections, slime-layer loss, "mis-growth" as the cancer analog, numb zones, failed-fusion scars, split scars. [C] (C)
- Pharmacopeia of ion-channel drugs: growth locks, growth primers, hygroscopic balms, antifungals. [C] (C)
- A recreational "shape-drunk" compound. [C] (C)
- Pain is the targeting map for attended healing, which is why local anesthesia cannot work. [C] (C)
- Healers as coaches. [C] (C)
- Dosing by lineage skill as well as body size. [C] (C)
- Graded impairment: willing a mitosis is the first ability lost under heavy sedation. [C] (C)
- Tolerance from the network turning sensitivity up. [C] (C)
- Withdrawal as involuntary rippling and uncontrolled growth. [C] (C)
- Held-form strain (bracing as living scaffolds) as the typical injury. [C] (C)
- Employer as pusher. [C] (C)
- Splitting to dump a hooked half fails because dependency splits unevenly too. [C] (C)
- Skin ointments and clothing as barrier coats, filter wraps and masks against smoke, spores, mining dust and factory air (pores take in air everywhere). [C] (L)
- Scars are poorly porous tissue, so scar removal also restores local gas exchange, and a scarred area can't be sealed safely. [C] (L)
- If the pain medicine flattens valence, addicted workers might speak with a distinctive calm but unpressured evenness, making addiction audible. [C] (L)
