## Overview

The regime controls dissent [mainly through forced splitting](#^summary): dissent is [cut out and destroyed when small](#^forced), and [exiled or imprisoned when it amounts to a whole person](#^exile). [Forced collapse is one of the most brutal tortures](#^torture). The ruler [restores lost skill by harvesting donors](#^harvest), [only from the city center](#^donors).

## Canon

- The regime controls dissent mainly through forced splitting: dissent is cut out and destroyed when small, and exiled or imprisoned when it amounts to a whole person. [Z] (B, D) ^summary

### Purge by mitose and forced splitting

- The state quietly uses coerced mitosis to extract dissent into a disposable half, so the outskirts are full of cast-off halves. Claude coined "purge by mitose"; Zach adopted it with enthusiasm. [C+] (B)
- Since involuntary splitting comes from letting go, the upper class can force subjects to split a certain way through controlled torture or trauma. [Z] (D)
- Forced splitting is a secret but common way of controlling dissent: forcibly split off just the piece or pieces holding dissent, then kill or incinerate that part. [Z] (D) ^forced
- Subjects with too much dissent to destroy, because the dissenting piece constitutes a mitosed person, are exiled to the outskirts or to a prison or camp, probably in the outskirts. [Z] (D) ^exile

### Forced collapse as torture

- Forcing stress-based dissolution is one of the most brutal tortures. It is not done just to kill (a blender is cheaper); it is done behind closed doors to split someone, or worse, until each part confesses. [Z] (D) ^torture
- Because the goop is forensically traceable, no ordinary murderer would try it; only the worst of the worst at the top would do it to their enemies. [Z] (D)

### Skill harvesting

- The ruler restores lost skill by fusing with a sacrificial skilled donor and splitting off the rest of the donor as waste. [Z] (C) ^harvest
- Donors must come from the city center, never the outskirts: inner-city Giglians share the ruler's ideology and are trained where the ruler needs it, while an outskirts Giglian carries the experience and truth of the predatory system, which would destabilize the faith. [Z] (C) ^donors
- Waste halves from harvests are handled by a mixture of exile and political imprisonment. [Z] (C)

### Economic control

- Manufactured food scarcity on the outskirts and controlled distribution in the middle rings (see [the city](the-city.md)). [Z] (C)

## Unconfirmed

- Interrogators can steer forced splits. [C] (D)
- Each fragment is a witness holding a different slice; several fragments "corroborate" a forced confession in a testimony-trusting court. [C] (D)
- Forced splitting framed as "treatment" for a failing baseline; survivors return calm with an unexplained memory hole, which reads as cured joy. [C] (D)
- Defenses: holding key substrate rigid under duress, or deliberately splitting first and stashing the dissent-holding piece in a resistance safe house to fuse back later. [C] (D)
- Records as a counter-check: a person whose memory contradicts the written record is a flagged purge survivor. [C] (D)
- Exiles are halves of loyal citizens still in the city, so separation prevents re-fusion. [C] (D)
- Camps hold inmates near the collapse threshold. [C] (C, D)
- Prisons control food and humidity because walls cannot hold a squishing, splitting body. [C] (C, D)
- Exiles are memory-washed by a further split. [C] (C, D)
- Waste halves of true-believer donors are ideal resistance messengers to the middle ring. [C] (C)
- Overshot interrogations leave lumpy goop that loyalists homogenize; a "too chronic" death becomes a tell. [C] (D)
