## Overview

A post-split Giglian [is not an infant](#^not-infant), so there is baseline re-education plus higher learning. [Clean, controlled splits need training that mostly only elites get](#^clean), but [dissidents can learn it too](#^dissidents). The proposed mechanism, [holding some tissue rigid while the rest relaxes](#^mechanism), is Claude's unconfirmed proposal [C].

## Canon

### Re-education and higher learning

- A post-mitosis Giglian is not an infant: they keep half the memories but lose half the learned skill, so there is baseline re-education plus higher learning beyond it. [Z] (C) ^not-infant
- A split can cost someone their livelihood: a good pianist and good tailor might keep the piano (a hobby) but lose the sewing muscle memory, and so their income. [Z] (C)
- Doctrine is not part of secular re-education. [Z] (F)

### Clean splitting

- Cleaner, controlled splits are possible but need training that mostly only elites get. [Z] (C) ^clean
- Elites learned it because the founder had to mitose to plan the city; leaders are given the skill as backup, workers are not. [Z] (C)
- It is a learned skill, so dissidents can learn it too, for example the protagonist on the full-mitosis path. [Z] (C) ^dissidents

## Unconfirmed

- Mechanism (from the split mechanics): selectively holding some tissue rigid while the rest relaxes. [C] (D) (was [C+], retagged 2026-10-29 after transcript check) ^mechanism

- Relearning is faster than first learning. [C] (C)
- Education runs alongside refeeding. [C] (C)
- Siblings are the best teachers. [C] (C)
- The intake assessment doubles as evidence of a greedy split. [C] (C)
- Re-education is also an indoctrination window. [C] (C)
- People keep a second marketable skill as insurance. [C] (C)
- Split clauses in employment contracts. [C] (C)
- Lineage "skill-banking". [C] (C)
- Clean-split practice requires real splits and fusing back, which needs a compatible partner, lots of food, time and safety. [C] (C, D)
- One's own halves are the ideal practice partners. [C] (C, D)
- The state's defenses are cost (food rationing) and surveillance rather than secrecy. [C] (C, D)
- Trained dissidents can resist forced splitting. [C] (C, D)
