## Overview

Because [memory dilutes with each split](#^dilutes), society depends on long-lived memory holders, history classes and re-education. Religion is [likely tied to record and family-history keeping](#^records), and a [history class is needed](#^history-class) because memory survives only so many splits.

## Canon

- Because memory dilutes with each split, Giglian society depends on long-lived memory holders (monks and recordkeepers), history classes, and re-education after every split. [Z] (C) ^dilutes

### Religion

- Religion is likely tied to record and family-history keeping, as churches kept family histories and printing began as religious. It also fills explanatory gaps before the scientific method. [Z] (C) ^records
- Doctrines on what splitting means are a city phenomenon (see [identity doctrines](identity-doctrines.md)). [Z] (F)

### History education

- A history class is needed because memory survives only so many splits; after enough, most cannot remember an era. It teaches the earlier corrupt courts as the rise and fall of nations. [Z] (C) ^history-class

## Unconfirmed

- The central theological gaps are identity after a split, death, "the Forgetting" of lost eras, and the founder. [C] (C)
- Slow-splitting monastic orders as clergy and living libraries. [C] (C)
- Greedy mitosis as sin and the cast-off half as a "lost soul". [C] (C)
- Fusion with a lost half as sacrament. [C] (C)
- The dead feeding the fungal farmland as a literal cycle. [C] (C)
- Heresy detectable by divergence from an orthodox lineage. [C] (C)
