## Overview

[One mechanism](#^one-mechanism), relaxing and re-holding pressure, underlies reproduction, depression and death. A split [needs a lot of food and intense concentration](#^requires), and the [psyche splits unevenly](#^psyche): a half keeps [about half the memories and loses about half the learned skill](#^half). Memory at a split is a recorded tension (T3).

## Canon

- One mechanism (relaxing and re-holding "pressure") underlies reproduction, depression and death; the only differences are control and fuel. [Z] (D) ^one-mechanism
- Requires eating a lot first (they keep growing, like alligators) so both halves have enough mass, then willing the split by intense concentration. [Z] (A) ^requires
- Mechanism: relax the pressure and coalesce back toward a blob, which mixes the substrate a bit as it redistributes, then two bodies condense out. [Z] (D)
- Normal mitosis feels like a controlled, meditative dissociation. [Z] (D)
- Psyche splits unevenly: most is shared, but specific memories, skills and personality aspects may end up in one half and be lost in the other, like the Rick and Morty split-personality episodes. Not a hivemind, more like clones. [Z] (A) ^psyche
- Later refinement: a split keeps about half the memories and loses about half the learned skill, so a post-split Giglian is not an infant. [Z] (C) ^half

> **Status: Disputed**
> T3: the earliest notes said both halves get the same memories (now [X], see History). Recorded in [tensions](../meta/tensions.md), unresolved.

- Giglians always know which memories are pre-split; those still feel like their own. [Z] (F)
- Memory survives only a limited number of splits; after enough, it dilutes so most cannot personally remember an era. [Z] (C)
- Splits need not be bilateral: a split can give one half both arms and the other both legs. [Z] (D)
- Clean (controlled) splits are a learned skill that mostly only elites get trained in. [Z] (C) See [education and clean splitting](../society/education-and-clean-splitting.md).
- Family history is based on the setting of the split; Giglians far from home slowly diverge from the groupthink. [Z] (A)
- Culture has a norm for what age or year to mitose, like when to have a baby. [Z] (C)

## Unconfirmed
