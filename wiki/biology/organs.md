## Overview

Zach has a [required list](#^required) of functions. The phonology work (beak, fused tongue, no nose) partly settles the mouth; see [phonology](../language/phonology.md).

Chat L sketched respiration. [The mouth is the exhaust](#^mouth-exhaust), outgassing byproducts and venting overpressure as an involuntary giggle. [Pressure circulates the diffused air, and that is the Joy mechanism](#^joy-circulation), so breathing is easier when joyous. [Giglians can close their mouth](#^mouth-close), but [holding your breath stops the filtration](#^no-filtration), and [holding it can feign joy, noticeably](#^feign-joy). No lungs, local pore sealing and carbonic-acid buildup are tentative ([Z?]); [pore intake](#^pores) was promoted to [Z] after chat M. The exact circulatory biology is still to be worked out (see T2 in [tensions](../meta/tensions.md)).

Chat M firmed up intake. [Baseline holding pressure is circulated through the porous tissue, fed by skin pores](#^holding-pressure). Giglians [have no gills or similar macro-scale structures, so they look smooth](#^no-gills), and Zach chose [pores as the intake system](#^pore-intake). The skin membrane as an entry point for invaders is tentative ([Z?]), and the body [keeps out large debris, then filters out or attacks invading cells](#^defense), whatever their world's [equivalent of bacteria and viruses](#^invaders) is. Skin oxygen exchange and evenly spread pores are tentative ([Z?]). The layered immune system (allorecognition and the rest) is Claude's unconfirmed proposal ([C]).

### Pores and intake (M0, M15–M17)

- A giglian has baseline holding pressure circulated through the porous body tissue, fed by pores in the skin. [Z] (M) ^holding-pressure
  - Bears on T2 (circulation) in [tensions](../meta/tensions.md).
- Per Zach's earlier idea about their evolution, their skin doesn't have a full cell wall and thus gets dehydrated, but the atmosphere is damp enough to support that. [Z] (M)

- Pores, so there's an actual intake system that works and doesn't break the ideas so far. [Z] (M) ^pore-intake
- Air would likely be gathered through pores in the "skin". [Z] (L) (was [Z?], promoted 2026-11-04) ^pores

- Pores are plenty smooth; structures like gills are macro-scale. [Z] (M)
- Skin diffusion can't feed a humanoid-sized, active body; skin-only breathing would mean a sluggish creature with low metabolism and heavy folds or frills. [C+] (M)
  - Claude (M14); Zach built on it in M15 ("so basically it would require gills or similar").

### From chat L: respiration (Claude)

- Air-filled foam is more plausible than pushed fluid, with fluid in a supporting role; the precedent is the insect tracheal system (no lungs, air piped to the tissues). [C] (L)
- Gas diffuses roughly ten thousand times faster than oxygen dissolved in water. [C] (L)
- Skin breathing alone fails at humanoid thickness, so pores need internal distribution through the porous foam. [C] (L)
- The humid atmosphere keeps wall-less cell surfaces moist and cuts water loss through the pores. [C] (L)
- A fluid carrier (an oxygen-binding analog) serves the dense core and brain-analog; cartilage gets by on diffusion. [C] (L)
- Collapse becomes suffocation: packed tissue loses the open foam channels, diffusion distances balloon and the core starves of oxygen. [C] (L)
- Mitosis relaxing pressure briefly throttles respiration, grounding soft, drowsy pre-split speech. [C] (L)
- The giggle as a relaxation oscillator: a pressurized reservoir venting through a valve; pulse rate rises with pressure above threshold, and low pressure means squeezing to cross it. [C] (L)
- The same pressure powers the foam muscles, so heavy exertion drains the gas budget speech draws on. [C] (L)
- One-way flow, pores in and mouth out, like a bird's flow-through lung; Giglians never stop talking to refill, so utterances are limited by pressure budget and waste clearance. [C] (L)
- Three vents from one opening: a maintenance vent (routine, sigh-like, clears waste), the overflow giggle (involuntary, releases surplus pressure) and speech (a shaped giggle). [C] (L)
- Pores sample whatever is in the air, so smoke, spores and gas act everywhere; with outer-ring factories and mining, air quality is a class issue. [C] (L)
- Joy as a feedback loop with two stable states: pressure drives circulation, circulation supports vigor, vigor keeps pressure up; falling is easy and climbing is hard; an overflow giggle works as a mild flush. [C] (L)
- Sealing costs attention (competing with growth, fighting and shaping speech), a long-sealed patch goes oxygen-poor, and low-pressure giglians can't hold seals long. [C] (L)
- Sealing is a budget: patch size (surface vs volume, the square-cube squeeze), pressure, and total throughput on the remaining open pores; it taxes the whole body, and failure is a gradual numbness, not a cliff. [C] (L)
- Damage to the internal network matters more than the skin: deep wounds or scars block channels, so a scarred area can't be sealed safely. [C] (L)
- A closed mouth is a pressure knob: pressure rises fast (deliberate inflation) but flushing stops, so inflation is a short-term burst and sustained baseline must come from circulation and joy. [C] (L)
- The urge to vent, like the human urge to breathe, is driven by waste buildup, not oxygen shortage. [C] (L)
- Acid buildup blurs bioelectric growth signaling, so a stifled or inflated giglian grows and reshapes worse. [C] (L)
- Speech doubles as flushing, so long conversation might feel good. [C] (L)
- Forced mouth closure as a torture method (slow chemical distress). [C] (L)
- Pressure and flow are two variables: real joy is high on both, while holding your breath raises pressure and kills flow. [C] (L)
- Tells of a faked baseline: taut, stiff texture; loud but jerky pulses with snatched vents; flat or sloppy fine control; strain growing over time. Faking suits brief public performances, staged rallies sound wrong to trained ears, and skilled elites use micro-vents. [C] (L)

### From chat M: respiration (Claude)

- Pores lead into the foam channels, and the channel walls are the real exchange surface: deep inside, with a huge total area; the outer skin works more like a gate and a first filter. [C] (M)
- The wall-less, damp membrane would line the whole internal network, which explains why the body needs humid air everywhere and not just a moist surface. [C] (M)
- Oxygen only penetrates about a millimeter into tissue by diffusion alone. [C] (M)
- An adult human has about 2 m² of skin but about 70 m² of lung surface; skin breathing supplies only about 1–2% of a human's oxygen. [C] (M)
- Animals that breathe only through skin, like lungless salamanders, are small, thin and slow; the Lake Titicaca frog grows baggy skin folds for extra area. [C] (M)
- The dense atmosphere helps: roughly twice Earth's pressure means roughly twice the uptake, but that's 2×, not the 35× the gap would need. [C] (M)
- Wall-less cells make the skin thinner and easier to cross, but thickness isn't the bottleneck; area and distance are. [C] (M)
- The speech and joy mechanism already needs bulk air inside the body, which skin diffusion can't move; pores are the intake side of the pump. [C] (M)
- The outer layer, a few millimeters deep, breathes directly through the skin; the core and brain-analog live off the pore and channel network. [C] (M)
- The pores and foam channels are in effect a lung spread through the whole body ("Gills turned inside out"); sealing a patch suffocates the tissue under it, not its surface. [C] (M)
- Pores can be tiny, at human-pore scale, visible only up close or when they clench. [C] (M)
- With evenly spread pores, every new growth brings its own intake, so reshaping doesn't starve the extremities, and sealing a patch cuts local supply only. [C] (M)

### From chat M: immunity (Claude)

- Immunity in layers along one entry route: at the pore, a reflex clench against irritants (smoke, spores, grit), like a blink, that costs oxygen. [C] (M)
- On the skin surface, a shed slime layer (mucus coat) that traps large debris; this would give the slime-layer-loss ailment ([medicine](../society/medicine-and-drugs.md)) a cause. [C] (M)
- In the channels, one-way flow sweeps trapped debris and dead cells to the exhaust; the maintenance vent doubles as a cough, sick giglians vent more, and their giggles sound wet or rough. [C] (M)
  - Zach, 2026-11-04 wrap-up review: no opinion on sickness symptoms yet; keep the topic open until it is discussed fully. Nothing about sickness is rejected.
- In the tissue, the immune system *is* allorecognition: any cell that isn't "self" gets engulfed (building on the [C] ancestry story in [ecology](../world/ecology-and-evolution.md)). [C] (M)
- A likely nastiest threat is a free-living cell that joins the body and passes the self-check instead of attacking it: the ancestry story's cheater turned into a disease, close to cancer. [C] (M)
