## Overview

Chronic deflation causes [involuntary "degenerate mitosis"](#^degenerate), which in the chronic case [cascades until the Giglian dies](#^cascade). [Dissolution is always uncontrolled mitosis](#^always-mitosis), and [chronic collapse leaves more homogeneous goop than forced collapse](#^forensics). The shared mechanism is on [mitosis](mitosis.md).

## Canon

- Chronic deflation (depression) lessens the ability to stay incorporated; letting go enough causes an involuntary split, "degenerate mitosis". Zach flagged this would change a lot about how the underclass works. [Z] (D) ^degenerate
- Chronic case: random splitting continues until cohesion is lost and the Giglian dies, once pieces lack faculties, mass and fuel. It looks like early embryonic cell division in reverse: smaller and smaller weakened husks that cannot support their own weight, shrivel and dry out into roughly a chunky powder. [Z] (D) ^cascade
- Wall-less cells in the cascade burst under their own weight from lack of nutrient and support. [Z] (D)
- Dissolution is always uncontrolled mitosis down to goop, never a separate process. [Z] (D) ^always-mitosis
- Collapse sequence: intentional modifications drop, then the base form, leaving a ballish blob with a strained smiley face (baseline expression is a smile, they are far below baseline). The ball looks like the normal head but blobbier and larger, since it holds the whole body's mass. Worse still, form is lost and they split to death. [Z] (D)
- Starvation alone can also cause the collapse to a ball. [Z] (D)
- The head is the last form to deform when a giglian disintegrates/deflates their body; first would be the attended growths. [Z] (M) ^head-last
- Personal collapse through depression is akin to suicide; forced collapse is akin to extreme shock causing a fatal stroke. [Z] (D) ^akin-suicide
- Suicide (self-inflicted death) is a separate class from depressive degenerate mitosis, and weighs more than for humans because it ends descendants' experience too. [Z] (F) ^suicide-class

Note (Zach, first ingest): the two lines above are compatible, not a tension. "akin to suicide" is an analogy about moral weight; "separate class" is a taxonomy.

- Forensics: chronic collapse leaves more homogeneous goop than forced collapse (like blood-splatter analysis). [Z] (D) ^forensics

## Unconfirmed

- If the head holds its structure longest, it keeps its own channels open longest, which helps explain why it's the last thing to go. [C] (M)
- Whether feeding revives a ball distinguishes starvation from pressure failure. [C] (D)
- Dried remains feed the fungi. [C] (D)
- Acute goop keeps lumps of frame and trained tissue. [C] (D)
- Fuel state of cells as a second forensic reading. [C] (D)
