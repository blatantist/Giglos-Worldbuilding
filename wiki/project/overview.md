## Overview

Giglos has three strands that feed each other: [worldbuilding](#^strand-lore), [game design](#^strand-game) and [agent AI](#^strand-ai), the last being the [active build focus](#^status-goap). Which chat each code refers to is in [sources](../meta/sources.md).

Note for readers (untagged in the source, applies to all chats): Zach corrects Claude often and many Claude extrapolations were walked back (see [rejected ideas](../meta/rejected-ideas.md)); weight [Z] lines far above [C] lines.

## Canon

### Strands

- Worldbuilding (lore): the Giglians, a slime-like, mitosis-reproducing species, their biology, ecology, psychology, society and a stratified city-state ruled by "the Founder". [Z] (A, C, D, E) ^strand-lore
- Game design: an RPG about a cast-off half of the Founder lineage toppling the regime, with multiple endgame routes like Fallout: New Vegas, but explicitly not a Bethesda-style RPG. [Z] (B, C, E) ^strand-game
- Agent AI: GOAP agents with blackboard sharing, belief modeling, persuasion and verbal acts, where language is only a renderer over the simulation. Currently the active build focus. [Z] (F, G, M2) ^strand-ai

### Long-term deliverables named by Zach

- A codex or artbook of Giglos, plus designs for the planet's other species and flora. [Z] (A, M3)
- A full Giglian conlang developed alongside history and narrative, Tolkien-style. [Z] (F)
- The game itself. [Z] (B, C)

### Status as of late Oct 2026

- GOAP agent work was on hiatus and is active again; the next step is a staged toy society environment building up to verbal communication. [Z] (G, M2) ^status-goap
- Species name is a placeholder pending phonology. [Z] (E)
- Zach was playing Disco Elysium as research ("homework"). [Z] (B)

### Design temperament

- Keeps systems deliberately decoupled, for example the skill-point economy is separate from the undecided dialogue and skill-check system. [Z] (B)
- Notes the setting is an unintentionally cartoonish take on human positivity, with implicit commentary on class hierarchy and mental health. [Z] (D)

## Unconfirmed

- Prefers scientifically grounded mechanisms that do explanatory work over arbitrary rules. [C] (A) (was [Z] (A, C), retagged 2026-10-29 after transcript check)
