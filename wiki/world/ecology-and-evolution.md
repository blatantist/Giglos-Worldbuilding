## Overview

The aim is a feasible account of [speciation among asexual Giglian-like organisms](#^goal). The [species boundary is mergeability](#^mergeability) ([C+]: Claude's proposal, which Zach liked), [dominant flora are fungi](#^fungi), and a [genome fixes the catalog of parts](#^vocab) an individual can arrange. Most of the deep-time mechanism is still Claude's unconfirmed proposal.

## Canon

- Zach's goal here is to work out how speciation feasibly happens among asexual Giglian-like organisms, in order to build the wider ecology of Giglos. [Z] (C) ^goal
- Species boundary = mergeability. Tissue fusion compatibility between lineages defines a species. Zach likes this because it ties back to his old mycelium-healing idea. [C+] (C) (was [Z], retagged 2026-10-29 after transcript check) ^mergeability
- Dominant flora are alien mushrooms and fungi. [Z] (C) ^fungi
- Deep-time lineage of mobile "fungi": free-floating bacteria-like cells, slime-mold-like organisms, proto-plants, many lineages rising and falling over billions of years, larger multicellular slime or fungus-like swimmers and sedentary filter feeders, then plants and animals on land. Order not fixed. [Z] (C)
- Very humid atmosphere is accepted as implied by the ecology. [Z] (C) ^humid

> **Status: Disputed**
> T6: the city's outer ring ends in open desert ([the city](../society/the-city.md)). Recorded in [tensions](../meta/tensions.md), unresolved.

- Genome as vocabulary: each species' genome fixes which organs and cell types it can grow (a bird analog grows bird eyes and wings; a Giglian grows a Giglian eye, arm and hand). Within that catalog the individual chooses arrangement and count. A bird analog could learn a more hand-shaped wing but stays bounded by its biology. [Z] (D) ^vocab
- Heredity of learned skill is real for them because they physically inherit the substrate holding experience; Zach agreed this looks Lamarckian but is true given their function. [Z] (C)

## Unconfirmed

- Speciation via niche ecotypes (plasticity is costly, so low-energy niches favor slow or sessile cousins). [C] (C)
- Mergeability via allorecognition genes. [C] (C)
- "culture before genome" via the Baldwin effect. [C] (C)
- Somatic mosaic mutations split unevenly at mitosis, so genetic and psychic divergence share one mechanism. [C] (C)
- Life on Giglos organized along walled vs wall-less and aggregate vs syncytial axes; walled lineages become the sessile mushroom flora, wall-less lineages stay mobile and evolve an internal cartilage lattice. [C] (C)
- Ancestry story: aggregators evolve allorecognition to police cheaters; walled fungal flora colonize land first and build humid soil; wall-less giants arrive late into moist understory. [C] (C)
- Ball resting form as a water-saving minimum-surface shape. [C] (C)
- Dry zones as speciation barriers. [C] (C)
- Cooling is hard in saturated air. [C] (C)
- Fog favors big dark eyes, sound and electroreception. [C] (C)
- Combustion is hard, so tech leans hydraulic, steam and bio-battery. [C] (C)
- Fungi as Giglians' shared lineage (as Earth fungi are closer to animals). [C] (C)
- Baroque fungal sexual systems contrasting Giglian asexuality. [C] (C)
- A Cordyceps-like growth-hijacking parasite. [C] (C)
- Fused-lineage chimeras. [C] (C)
- Hybridization by absorption. [C] (C)
- Forced incompatible fusion as an attack (necrotic scar). [C] (C)
