## Overview

The game is [an RPG where a cast-off half of the Founder lineage works to topple the regime](#^summary). The [working plot](#^plot) has the leader split off a "weak" half that becomes the protagonist, [the most capable dissenter](#^capable). Later lore says [the original founder no longer exists](../society/government-and-founder.md); whether the protagonist is therefore half of the diluted ruler is [Claude's unconfirmed reading](#^refine) [C]. Premise vs Founder lore is tension T1, unresolved. The motive for the split leans on purge by mitose, which is [C+].

Chat N names the protagonist [Glasgo](#^glasgo). [A political feud forced the "founder" tip to mitose into a safe figurehead](#^feud), along [humanity vs fascist resolve](#^axis): [the founder got the memory and experience, Glasgo got the skill but has to learn to use it](#^glasgo-split). Because [the tip had gone unchanged for so long, the split-off part was a full being](#^full-being), and [revolutionaries captured and rehabilitated it](#^rehabilitated).

## Canon

- The game is an RPG where a cast-off half of the Founder lineage works to topple the regime by one of four paths, with endgame routes inspired by Fallout: New Vegas but not built like a Bethesda RPG. [Z] (B, C, E) ^summary
- Working plot: over the city's history the head of the city-state-corporation kept a chain of command by split distance. The one remaining closest to the first split holds the most lived experience of the world before, and so still carries some "humanity", deemed a weakness. They mitose the "good" and "bad" parts apart, producing one perfect fascist leader and one "weak" half that wants change (escape from groupthink, civil liberty). The weak half is cast aside and becomes the protagonist. [Z] (B) ^plot

> **Status: Disputed**
> T1: later lore says the original founder no longer exists and the Founder is a diluted lineage tip ([government](../society/government-and-founder.md)). Recorded in [tensions](../meta/tensions.md), unresolved.

- The motive for that split was not worked out; Zach liked purge by mitose (the state's own tool turned inward) as the precedent. [C+] (B)
- The protagonist is the most capable dissenter because they are half of a full founder-level person, not the marginal waste of dissenters further down the chain. [Z] (B) ^capable
- Player agency over where the plot goes (revolution or otherwise). [Z] (B)
- The goal of toppling the regime is like Fallout: New Vegas, but not a Bethesda-type RPG. [Z] (C)

### Glasgo (N18–N20)

- Glasgo is the protagonist's canon name. [Z] (N) ^glasgo
- The protagonist's mitose from the founder was essentially: the founder got the memory and experience, Glasgo got the skill, but has to learn how to use it, hence the skill tree. [Z] (N) ^glasgo-split
  - Not a tension with [mitosis ^half](../biology/mitosis.md#^half): that mitose was deliberate and targeted ([mitosis ^targeted](../biology/mitosis.md#^targeted)).
- The split is still humanity vs fascist resolve. [Z] (N) ^axis
- The creation of Glasgo was due to a political feud that decided the current "founder" tip (split closest to the founder) had too many counter-doctrinal ideas and too much ability, so forced them to mitose into a safe figurehead. [Z] (N) ^feud
  - Bears on [^plot](#^plot), where the split is described from the leader's side; see [tensions](../meta/tensions.md) T1.
- For a long time, based on tradition, the founder tip went unchanged to stay true to their ideals, so the part split off is large enough to constitute a full being. [Z] (N) ^full-being
- The few revolutionaries/spies that did exist captured and rehabilitated the thrown-out waste into their chosen one, Glasgo. [Z] (N) ^rehabilitated
