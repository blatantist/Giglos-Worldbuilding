## Overview

The game is [an RPG where a cast-off half of the Founder lineage works to topple the regime](#^summary). The [working plot](#^plot) has the leader split off a "weak" half that becomes the protagonist, [the most capable dissenter](#^capable). Later lore says [the original founder no longer exists](../society/government-and-founder.md); whether the protagonist is therefore half of the diluted ruler is [Claude's unconfirmed reading](#^refine) [C]. Premise vs Founder lore is tension T1, unresolved. The motive for the split leans on purge by mitose, which is [C+].

## Canon

- The game is an RPG where a cast-off half of the Founder lineage works to topple the regime by one of four paths, with endgame routes inspired by Fallout: New Vegas but not built like a Bethesda RPG. [Z] (B, C, E) ^summary
- Working plot: over the city's history the head of the city-state-corporation kept a chain of command by split distance. The one remaining closest to the first split holds the most lived experience of the world before, and so still carries some "humanity", deemed a weakness. They mitose the "good" and "bad" parts apart, producing one perfect fascist leader and one "weak" half that wants change (escape from groupthink, civil liberty). The weak half is cast aside and becomes the protagonist. [Z] (B) ^plot

- The motive for that split was not worked out; Zach liked purge by mitose (the state's own tool turned inward) as the precedent. [C+] (B)
- The protagonist is the most capable dissenter because they are half of a full founder-level person, not the marginal waste of dissenters further down the chain. [Z] (B) ^capable
- Player agency over where the plot goes (revolution or otherwise). [Z] (B)
- The goal of toppling the regime is like Fallout: New Vegas, but not a Bethesda-type RPG. [Z] (C)
