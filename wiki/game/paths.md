## Overview

There are four main paths, distinguished by where the protagonist's bodies come from: [solo recruitment](#^solo), [mitosis + recruitment](#^mixed), [balanced mitosis](#^balanced) and [unbalanced mitosis](#^unbalanced). On every path [the player picks one half to continue as at each split](#^half-choice), and [infiltrating the center is required](#^infiltration).

**Alias:** "neutral" path or build is the pre-four-paths name for mitosis + recruitment (Zach, first ingest). Older claim lines that say "neutral" refer to that path.

## Canon

### The four main paths

- Solo recruitment: bodies come from recruits only; never mitose. [Z] (E) ^solo
- Mitosis + recruitment (alias: neutral): balanced mitosis plus recruiting. [Z] (E) ^mixed
- Balanced mitosis: own halves only, no recruitment. [Z] (E) ^balanced
- Unbalanced mitosis: make yourself powerful with an army of peons. The "evil" route; may involve recruitment, likely through fear. [Z] (E) ^unbalanced

### Rules attached to the paths

- Infiltrating the center is required on every path; recruitment gives easier access via middle-ring support. [Z] (E) ^infiltration
- On every path, at each mitosis the player chooses which half to continue as; the other half becomes an ordinary GOAP NPC. There is no swapping between halves (no psychic link in lore, and swapping would act as teleporting, break up the game, and buff the neutral path over solo recruitment). [Z] (E) ^half-choice
- Skill-tree flavor by path: neutral builds mix some mitosis with using others for numbers; full recruitment or solo play gives more offensive skills; full mitosis gives abilities specific to splitting and regenerating well. [Z] (C)
- Clean splitting can be learned by the protagonist on the full-mitosis path. [Z] (C)
- Two macro routes to victory: revolution vs peaceful council takeover (see [resistance](../society/resistance.md)). [Z] (F)
- A solitary monk recordkeeper who remembers the pre-city era is a planned encounter. [Z] (C)

## Unconfirmed

- The unbalanced path mirrors the founder; success there means becoming the next Founder, subject to the same decay. [C] (E)
- The abandoned half can return as ally, rival, or regime captive who testifies against you. [C] (E)
- Choosing a half under a clean split (informed partition) vs a dirty split (partly random gamble). [C] (E)

## Open questions

## History

- This four-path set supersedes the earlier spectrum of recruit-only, self-mitose-only and a neutral middle. [X] (B, C)
- Swapping control between mitosed halves, RTS-style, with a 2 to the n control budget [X] (E). Replaced by: choose one half to continue as; the other becomes an ordinary NPC.
